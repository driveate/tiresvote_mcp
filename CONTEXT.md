# TiresVote

A catalog of tire models, their variants, and published evidence about their
properties.

## Language

**Tire brand**:
The trade name that groups tire models together.
_Avoid_: a vehicle make.

**Tire model**:
A named line of tires from one brand, represented by a set of variants.
_Avoid_: a vehicle model, a tire size, an individual stock item.

**Tire size**:
The geometric designation of a tire, e.g. `225/45R17`, without mandatory load
and speed indices.

**Tire variant**:
A version of a model in a specific size with load and speed indices and
attributes such as XL. One size may correspond to several variants.
_Avoid_: a vehicle trim.

**Size availability**:
The presence of a known current variant of the model with the requested size.
_Avoid_: warehouse stock, proven compatibility with a vehicle.

**Fitment**:
The compliance of a wheel or tire with the requirements of a specific vehicle
and its trim. This is a separate concept from size availability for a tire
model.

**Market region**:
The territory for which a model is presented in the TiresVote catalog.
_Avoid_: automatically equating it with the Wheel Fitment market reference.

**Performance category**:
The class of a tire by intended use, season, and road conditions.

**Brand price segment**:
The positioning of a brand, e.g. premium or economy.
_Avoid_: the current price of a specific tire.

**CoreScore**:
TiresVote's aggregated model score based on reviews and professional tests.
_Avoid_: the score of an individual test, popularity, a guarantee of safety.

**Popularity**:
A separate measure of a model's recognition and demand.
_Avoid_: tire quality, CoreScore.

**Professional test**:
A comparison of a set of models under conditions and a size set by the
organizer. Places and scores belong to that specific test.

**Tested size**:
The size in which a test was conducted; a participant having other sizes does
not carry the measurement results over to them.

**Published reason**:
A claim about a model's strength or weakness with a source of confirmation.
_Avoid_: an unconditional fact about any variant of the model.

**Related material**:
An article, video, external link, or comparison result related to a model.

**Successor**:
A model released to replace another model. A successor does not necessarily
have all of its sizes.
